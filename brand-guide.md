# Future Skills Academy — Brand & Design System

## Company

Future Skills Academy (futureskillsacademy.ai) is a Stockholm-based academy that trains the
skills that compound in an AI-native world. Our thesis: AI made execution cheap, so the human
layer — judgment, agency, taste, trust, and clarity — is the scarce asset. We teach five tracks
(Member of Technical Staff, High Agency, Taste, Relationships, Communication), each an eight-week
curriculum of dense lessons, real-stakes practice, and a shipped capstone. The open curriculum is
free; revenue comes from B2B workshops, guided team cohorts, and upskilling partnerships.

Audience: founders, engineers, and leaders at companies adopting AI — and the executives who buy
training for their teams. The brand must feel credible to a CTO and a CFO at once.

## Brand personality

Quiet confidence. Rigorous, warm, editorial — a serious academy, not a SaaS landing page.
Think: a well-set book with one strong brushstroke of color. We persuade with clarity and
restraint, never hype. No exclamation marks, no gradients-on-everything, no startup clichés.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Bone (background) | `#fbf8f4` | Page background |
| Rice | `#f4eee7` | Alternate section background |
| Paper | `#fffaf6` | Cards, panels |
| Plum (ink) | `#332432` | Body text, headlines, dark panels |
| Carmine (accent) | `#bd2848` | The one accent: links, CTAs, emphasis, labels |
| Carmine deep | `#9c1f3b` | Accent hover |
| Rose | `#c96a70` | Secondary accent (sparingly) |
| Blush | `#e8b7ad` | Soft accents, borders on dark |
| Mist | `#f2dcd4` | Highlight backgrounds (e.g. callouts) |
| Line | `rgba(51,36,50,0.14)` | Hairline borders |

Rule: mostly warm neutrals + plum ink; carmine is the single voice of emphasis and means
"look here / act here." Never use carmine decoratively at scale.

## Typography

- **Display / headlines:** Fraunces (weights 400–600, italic for emphasis words).
  Large, tight line-height (1.08–1.2), slightly negative letter-spacing.
- **Body / UI:** Helvetica Neue / Inter / system sans. 15.5–17px body, line-height 1.65.
- **Labels / kickers:** sans, 12px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.28–0.34em, carmine.
- Signature move: one italic carmine word inside a plum serif headline
  (e.g. "The skills that *compound* when AI does the rest.").

## Layout & spacing

- Max content width 1180px; generous section padding (~90–160px top).
- Whitespace is a material: airy, calm, editorial. When in doubt, add space, remove elements.
- Cards: 14–22px radius, paper background, hairline border, subtle lift on hover.
- Buttons: pill-shaped (999px radius); filled carmine primary, hairline-outlined ghost secondary.

## Logo

- Primary mark: rose-and-carmine watercolor brushstroke above the "FUTURE SKILLS / ACADEMY"
  wordmark (serif caps, letterspaced).
- Use the transparent-background version on our bone backgrounds.
- The brushstroke alone works as a standalone graphic element (see social image).
- Favicon: plum rounded square with a single carmine stroke.

## Voice

- Declarative, concrete, short sentences. Lead with the point.
- Aphoristic headlines ("When intelligence is abundant, judgment is the bottleneck.").
- Eternal-skill framing: every claim pairs a timeless principle with its 2026 practice.
- Never: hype, emoji, exclamation marks, filler ("unlock", "supercharge", "revolutionize").
