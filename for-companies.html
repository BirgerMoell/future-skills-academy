The workshop day

What a day with us looks like

09:00 — Mindset & division of labor

Where your hours actually go

Each person audits their week against the machines-draft/humans-decide line. Teams typically find 40–60% of hours on the wrong side. We set personal delegation targets.

10:30 — Specification & context

Briefing machines like seniors

Live drills: turning vague tickets from your real backlog into specs an AI agent executes correctly first try — and learning to see why the vague version fails.

13:00 — Agents & verification

Hands on your own codebase

Pairs run coding agents on real tasks from your repository, with verification checklists and review discipline. The afternoon’s diffs ship if your team approves them — that’s the point.

15:30 — Evals & the way forward

Making quality measurable

We build the first eval set for one of your AI features, then close with the before/after assessment and a 30-day habit plan your leads own.
