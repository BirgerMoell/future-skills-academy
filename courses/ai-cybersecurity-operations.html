AI & Cybersecurity Operations

For the operators who will make AI usable without making organizations fragile: monitoring, policy, incident response, risk, and secure automation.

FormatPlanned full-time
ModeLab + LIA
LanguageEnglish
StatusPlanned YH concept
Essence

AI must be governed by people who understand both systems and risk.

The course centers on operational judgment: how to use AI in security work, how to secure AI-enabled workflows, and when a human must remain in control.

  • Use AI for alert triage, log analysis, documentation, and incident response support.
  • Threat-model AI workflows, prompts, data flows, suppliers, and model behavior.
  • Create lightweight governance aligned with GDPR, NIS2-style resilience, and EU AI Act readiness.
  • Run tabletop exercises and write response playbooks that real teams can follow.
