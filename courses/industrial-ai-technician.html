Industrial AI Technician

For hands-on technical people bringing AI into production, maintenance, energy, logistics, quality work, and physical operations.

FormatPlanned full-time
ModeWorkshop + LIA
LanguageEnglish
StatusPlanned YH concept
Essence

The future of AI is not only on screens. It is in rooms where things move.

This course connects data, automation, and practical operations so students can improve real technical environments without losing sight of safety, reliability, and people.

  • Collect, clean, and interpret operational data from machines, processes, and sensor streams.
  • Prototype anomaly detection, predictive maintenance, and quality-inspection workflows.
  • Connect AI outputs to dashboards, human review, work orders, and automation boundaries.
  • Explain reliability, safety, and maintenance implications to both engineers and managers.
01

Sense

Industrial data, sensors, time series, quality signals, production context, and practical statistics.

02

Automate

Automation concepts, edge/cloud patterns, dashboards, alerts, work orders, and human review loops.

03

Stabilize

Reliability, safety, maintenance, documentation, handover, and LIA projects with industrial partners.
