AI Automation Developer

For builders who turn messy internal workflows into reliable AI-enabled systems: agents, RAG, APIs, integrations, evaluation, and handover.

FormatPlanned full-time

Essence

Students leave with systems employers can inspect.

Every major assessment produces a working artifact: an automation, an evaluation report, a deployment plan, and documentation another team can maintain.

  • Build retrieval-augmented assistants for internal knowledge and support workflows.
  • Connect AI systems to databases, APIs, queues, CRMs, and business tools.
  • Evaluate quality, cost, latency, privacy, and failure modes before launch.
  • Document systems clearly enough for an employer to adopt or reject with confidence.
