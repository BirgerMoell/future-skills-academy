Future Skills Academy

Planned YH initiative for applied AI in Swedish working life.

YH

Planned YH application

Applied AI for people who will change real workflows.

Future Skills Academy is preparing a Swedish yrkeshögskola application focused on AI automation, harness engineering, orchestration, evaluation, and organizational understanding. The education is being shaped with employers and is not an approved YH programme until MYH has made a decision.

01

Build

Students create working AI systems, not only presentations about AI.

02

Evaluate

Every workflow must be tested for quality, cost, privacy, security, and failure modes.

03

Transfer

The point is adoption: handover, documentation, change work, and clear operational ownership.

Courses

Three possible directions for employer-led AI education.

01 AI Automation Developer Agents, RAG, integrations, workflow systems 02 AI & Cybersecurity Operations Secure adoption, monitoring, governance 03 Industrial AI Technician Production data, automation, resilient operations

The first application will be narrowed with the employer council so the final programme answers a specific labour-market need.

Arbetslivets medverkan

The programme is built around employers, LIA, and real briefs.

The employer council will help define course goals, prerequisites, LIA projects, guest lectures, assessment briefs, and the role profile students should be ready for after graduation.

Need

Applied AI roles that combine technical building with workflow analysis and organizational judgment.

LIA

Workplace learning with supervision, concrete project scopes, and feedback from real teams.

Governance

A ledningsgrupp with working-life majority will keep the education anchored in current demand.

Evidence

Formal avsiktsförklaringar are gathered through MYH Mina sidor as part of the application.
